# Newton Lab — Research Synthesis and Capability-Gap Audit

## Purpose and scope

This document audits selected completed Newton Lab research threads and the reusable APIs that support them. It is a repository-grounded synthesis, not a new experiment, literature review, or claim of scientific novelty. Evidence statements are limited to reports, source, tests, protocols, and outputs present in this workspace. Historical conclusions and statuses are preserved.

## Executive summary

Newton Lab now has typed numerical solvers for ODE initial-value problems, nonlinear algebraic systems, and boundary-value problems; parameter-sweep and sensitivity APIs; explicit equation and structure records; analysis, visualization, reporting, and a bounded discovery workflow. These are tested software capabilities, but they do not form an autonomous scientific discovery system. The discovery workflow consumes already-computed experiment records, derives a limited set of descriptors, compares declared equation metadata, and drafts hypotheses. It does not infer equations from data, perform symbolic mathematics, or validate an application in its target domain.

Research evidence is strongest for mathematical properties under stated model assumptions and numerical implementations checked against analytical or independent references. The recovery-scaling, Kuramoto, pitchfork-path, and non-normal studies are synthetic or model-specific. No independently measured engineering or financial application was validated in the audited material. The Kuramoto thread remains closed and inconclusive under its protocol; Phase 62's slower-ramp result remains inconclusive; Phase 64's gain result is restricted to its declared two-state matrices, Euclidean norm, and finite grids.

**Primary recommendation: pause new experiments and consolidate.** Phase 58 already adopted that policy for the recovery-scaling thread. Phases 60–64 add bounded evidence, not a qualified target or a reason to reopen closed questions. Preserve the current evidence and statuses; reconsider new work only when an independently measured target is qualified or a genuinely distinct falsifiable mechanism is identified.

## Evidence labels

- **A — analytic:** follows from a derivation under explicit equation, branch, and boundary assumptions.
- **B — numerical/software verification:** implementation or numerical output agrees with an analytical or independent reference, or passes a stated resolution check. This checks computation, not real-world model validity.
- **C — protocol-specific synthetic:** observed for declared generators, parameters, seeds, windows, noise, and decision rules.
- **D — cross-model/domain hypothesis:** a proposed or tested structural correspondence across models; application transfer remains hypothetical without target evidence.
- **E — independent empirical application:** evidence from an independently sourced and qualified real target. No E evidence was identified for the audited threads.

These labels are evidence descriptions, not a cumulative score. A failed threshold, a result contrary to expectation, an inconclusive interval, and a numerical failure mean different things.

## End-to-end discovery capability

The intended workflow is **existing results → behavioral characterization → structure matching → candidate hypothesis → evidence-aware report → target-domain validation**.

1. **Existing model and experiment results — implemented for supported families.** Solvers and `run_experiment` produce typed records; study-specific modules also create their own protocols/results. These entry points are not one universal execution API.
2. **Behavioral characterization — partially implemented.** `run_discovery` extracts selected recorded metrics and sweep changes, plus limited period/amplitude descriptors when trajectories were retained. It preserves source IDs, solver status, numerical acceptance, and missingness. It does not fit arbitrary dynamical laws or infer causal mechanisms.
3. **Mathematical structure matching — implemented as metadata comparison.** `compare_equations` and discovery matching compare declared classifications/features and recorded relationships. This is not symbolic-equivalence proving and does not discover structure from the data.
4. **Candidate cross-domain hypotheses — partially implemented.** Discovery can produce explicitly proposed, assumption-bearing hypotheses and test plans. `ApplicationHypothesis` fixes its evidence status to proposed and `validated_application` to false. Cross-system comparison helpers also create bounded findings. Neither path independently validates the target.
5. **Evidence-aware report — implemented.** Experiment reports, discovery Markdown, study-specific reports, provenance metadata, and reproducibility artifacts preserve limitations and numerical status for their respective workflows.
6. **Target-domain validation — absent in the audited evidence.** Phase 52 is `NO-GO`; Phase 32 remains `BLOCKED_AUTHORIZATION`. No audited engineering or financial application has the required independent target measurements and authorization evidence.

There is a usable, manually connected discovery workflow for already-recorded supported experiments, but **no complete end-to-end autonomous discovery pipeline**. Detecting a numerical behavior does not identify its generator; matching declared features does not prove a mathematical relationship; a mathematical mapping does not establish a physical model; and a proposed application is not target validation.

## Evidence-backed gaps

| Gap | Why it matters and affected work | Minimum useful response and completion evidence | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| No qualified independent target for the application claims audited here (**data/evidence gap**). | Prevents assessing whether idealized structures persist under real measurement, model error, and target-specific uncertainty. Phase 52 records no qualified engineering candidate; Phase 32 records unresolved financial authorization. | Do not build infrastructure now. Reconsider only after a named target, documented provenance/permission, defined measurements, and a held-out test are jointly available. Completion is a documented readiness gate plus target-specific protocol, not a positive result. | If no permitted, sufficiently informative target is available, stop application-validation work and keep synthetic claims bounded. |
| Discovery characterization and matching are intentionally limited (**scientific/architecture boundary**). | The current workflow uses selected metrics and recorded equation metadata; it cannot derive general laws or symbolic equivalence from raw output. This matters if a future user expects automatic discovery. | No change is presently justified: the API labels descriptors, unknowns, provenance, and hypotheses conservatively. Address only a specific supported workflow that demonstrates an actual descriptor/matching failure. Test with a counterexample and require the workflow to retain uncertainty or reject. | Stop if the requested behavior requires unsupported automatic scientific inference without a defined falsifiable test. |
| Study-specific runners have differing protocols/artifacts (**reusability boundary**). | Protocol/result formats and runner interfaces vary by study. This increases work when comparing and consolidating evidence but has not blocked completed bounded studies. | Prefer a documentation-level map of inputs, outputs, and manifests—this audit—over a new framework. Consider implementation only when a repeated concrete task cannot be done simply with existing APIs. | Stop at a traceable, reproducible manual workflow; do not generalize runners preemptively. |

These are boundaries and prerequisites, not a list of features that must all be implemented. Optional machine learning, symbolic algebra, databases, dashboards, or generalized orchestration are not justified by the audited evidence.

## Recommendation

**Pause and consolidate.** This is consistent with the explicit Phase 58 closure decision. The newer Kuramoto and pitchfork results are inconclusive on their stated questions, while Phase 64 establishes a bounded matrix-family result. None supplies independent application evidence or changes the authorization/qualification gates. Another adjacent synthetic benchmark or generalized framework would add less decision-relevant evidence than preserving clear links between protocols, numerical findings, assumptions, and current status.

The useful stopping condition is met when this synthesis and the linked reports let a researcher trace each conclusion to its model, protocol, implementation, and output. No additional implementation or experiment is proposed here. Reconsider only on a separately reviewed request that identifies either a qualified independent target or a distinct mechanism with a result capable of changing the evidence. Keep future work out of the closed Kuramoto and recovery-scaling threads unless a genuinely new question is justified; this recommendation is not authorization for a new phase.

## Limitations of this audit

- This was a targeted inspection, not an exhaustive reread or independent recalculation of every result in Phases 1–64.
- Foundational APIs were inspected in their source and relevant architecture documentation; the full test suite was not rerun because no code changed.
- Numerical values and statuses are attributed to the reports and artifacts cited above. This audit did not rerun simulations or independently recompute their statistics.
- “No independent empirical evidence identified” refers to the audited workspace, not all evidence that may exist elsewhere.
- No literature search or novelty assessment was performed.
