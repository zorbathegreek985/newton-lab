# Scientific principles

## Reproducibility

Record the configuration, input data provenance, software versions, and random
seed used for computational work. A seed helps reproduce pseudorandom runs; it
does not guarantee bit-for-bit identity across hardware, library versions, or
parallel execution.

## Validation and uncertainty

Validate inputs and assumptions before interpreting outputs. Check units,
domains, numerical ranges, and limiting cases where relevant. Report uncertainty
and sensitivity to data, parameters, and modeling choices instead of presenting
an estimate as exact when it is not.

## Evidence and language

Distinguish established physical laws, mathematical analogies, hypotheses, and
findings supported by experiments. Similar equations or behavior do not by
themselves show that two systems share a physical mechanism. State what evidence
supports a claim and what remains untested. Financial simulations are exposed to
data and model limitations; no strategy should be called profitable without
rigorous, appropriately controlled evidence.
